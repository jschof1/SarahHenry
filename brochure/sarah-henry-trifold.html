Welcome

Every story deserves to be beautifully told.

Each life story is unique, and I believe life's most precious moments deserve to be celebrated, commemorated, and reflected upon in a way that feels truly right for you.

Whether you are planning a wedding, a funeral, a baby naming or a vow renewal, I take the time to get to know you, listening carefully to your story or the story of your loved one. From our first contact, I am fully invested in creating a ceremony that feels authentic, meaningful and completely personal.

The Journey

How we work together

1

We meet

A relaxed, no-obligation chat, in person, by phone or over video, so we can get to know each other and talk through what you have in mind.

2

I listen

I gather your story, the people, the memories, the values and the small details that make this moment yours, so the ceremony feels genuinely personal.

3

I craft

I carefully write your ceremony from scratch and share it with you to review. You can revise it as many times as you need until every word feels right.

4

I deliver

On the day I make sure everything flows seamlessly, so you can simply be present with the people you love and enjoy the moment you are marking.
